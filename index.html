Boggle Trainer

Welcome! How this works

Boggle Trainer helps you get better at word-finding boards (like the Netflix Boggle game) by tracking which words you find and which you miss.

  1. Play: pick a board size and time, then press Start game. Swipe your finger (or drag the mouse) across touching letters – including diagonals – to spell a word. Lift to submit. Each tile can be used once per word.
  2. Results: when time is up you'll see every word on the board, the ones you found, and the ones you missed. Tap any word for its definition.
  3. Learn: missed words are collected in the Learn tab, along with word families, patterns and letter combos, so you can study the words you keep missing. Turn on Smart board to get boards containing those words.
  4. Stats: see your progress over time. Invalid swipes are saved under Rejected (tap ✕ if it was just a misswipe).
  5. Simulate / Tools: find out which words appear most often, enter a board from Netflix to play or log it, and manage profiles and data.

Tip: Practice (top right) lets you play without affecting your stats. Everything is stored on this device only.
